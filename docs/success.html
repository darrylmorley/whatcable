How to activate

1
Download WhatCable and drag it to your Applications folder. Skip this if you already have it installed.
2
Check your email for a message from hello@whatcable.uk with your licence key.
3
Right-click WhatCable in the menu bar and click Licence.
4
Paste your key into the licence field and activate.
5
Pro features unlock immediately. Works on up to 2 Macs.
